# Learn Pricing Analytics with Phoebe

Pricing as a commercial decision backed by data: what a price change does to margin, where list
price leaks before it reaches the bank, how to measure willingness to pay, and how to tell a
discount's real lift from the season it rode on. Ten 45-minute sessions in two tracks, by Phoebe Fu.

- **Business (4 sessions, no code):** price is the biggest lever; value, cost or competitor; where
  the money leaks; reading a pricing recommendation.
- **Analyst (6 sessions, hands-on in the browser):** the pricing data model; measuring willingness
  to pay; deciding with a borrowed elasticity; promo lift and the dip after; tiers, bundles and price
  fences; the promo and discount lab.

Open `index.html` to start. Every widget computes in the browser from `assets/pra-sample.js`.

## Data

- **Real rows:** product x month units and mean item prices, July 2017 to January 2018, derived from
  the Olist panel built for Learn Economics with Phoebe. Source: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset
  by Olist, Kaggle, licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Derived numbers only; non-commercial use.
- **Constructed:** Kettle & Co, a fictional tea brand, with its prices, costs, wholesale accounts
  and survey answers. Seeded and labelled wherever it appears.

The elasticity is taken as an input and never re-estimated here; estimation lives in Learn Economics
with Phoebe.
