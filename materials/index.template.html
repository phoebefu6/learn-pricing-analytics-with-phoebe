Learn Pricing Analytics with Phoebe

A price change is the one lever that lands on profit whole, and the one most often set by habit. These ten sessions treat pricing as a commercial decision backed by data. Four Business sessions are for the people who set and defend prices: why price is the biggest lever, where a price should come from, where the money leaks between the list and the bank, and how to read a pricing recommendation. Six Analyst sessions are for the people who measure it: the pricing data model, willingness to pay, deciding with an elasticity somebody else estimated, promo lift and the month after, tiers and the price-volume-mix bridge, and a lab that runs Black Friday 2017 on real marketplace rows. The running brand, Kettle & Co, is constructed.

For commercial leads, category managers, founders and finance partners who sign off a price. The profit line and the discount break-even, value against cost-plus, the pocket price waterfall and band, and a checklist for any "raise prices" deck, with what regulators have said about prices set from personal data.
